About Vusmafashion Styles

About

Vusmafashion Styles is a creative direction and production design studio focused on premium visual storytelling, fashion-forward concepts, campaign execution and brand image development.

The studio has collaborated with globally recognised and culturally relevant brands including Vodacom, Aperol, Vuse, Jameson, Flying Fish, Cars.co.za, Makro and The Wife telenovela, delivering elevated visual experiences and compelling bold visuals.

Across commercial campaigns and production environments, the creative has also worked as Head of Department (HOD) within the art department, contributing to the execution, visual coordination and overall creative direction of large-scale commercial projects.
